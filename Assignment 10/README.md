1. Description
My database compiles every video game that I can remember having played within the last 6ish years. Every entry has the title, genre, publisher, year of release, version (if live-service), and platform(listed as "Multiple" if available on more than one platform). This helps me look at this information in a compact form and answer how many and which specific games I have played under specific parameters.
